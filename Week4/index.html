Explore the Solar System

A journey through the cosmos, one planet at a time.

The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma and accounts for about 99.86% of the total mass of the Solar System. Without it, life on Earth would not exist.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. Known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface, Mars is home to Olympus Mons, the tallest volcano in the entire solar system at nearly 22 kilometers high.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System. Its Great Red Spot is a storm that has raged for hundreds of years and is wider than the entire Earth. Jupiter has at least 95 known moons orbiting it.

The Milky Way

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System. It is a barred spiral galaxy estimated to hold between 100 and 400 billion stars, and our Solar System sits roughly 26,000 light-years from its center.
